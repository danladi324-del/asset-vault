Wave-Function Collapse & Superposition

Two views of the same underlying phase: a double-slit buildup, where the fringe pattern only emerges from many independent detections, and a Bloch sphere, where that phase is the azimuth of a two-level qubit's state vector. The φ control below drives both.

Each dot is an independent detection event, sampled from |A + B|². Fringes appear only in the statistics of many — no single particle carries the pattern.
